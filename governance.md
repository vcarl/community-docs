### Corporate Structure Options

1. **Informal Community Project**

   - Simplest to start but offers limited liability protection and formal authority
   - Consider for initial phases while testing viability

2. **Nonprofit Organization (501c3)**

   - Good for educational missions and enables tax-deductible donations
   - Requires board formation and ongoing compliance requirements

3. **Benefit Corporation or LLC**
   - Provides liability protection while allowing for mission-driven focus
   - Easier to establish than nonprofit but still provides legal structure
   - Can transition to nonprofit later if direction shifts

### Operating Agreement Components

1. **Purpose and Mission Statement**

   - Define scope boundaries (what's in/out of your documentation efforts)
   - Articulate relationship to the product ecosystem (independent but not adversarial)

2. **Decision Rights Matrix**

   - Specify what decisions require full oversight team consensus vs. individual authority
   - Create clear escalation paths for contentious issues

3. **Financial Structure**

   - How will any funds be managed (volunteer basis vs. paid positions eventually?)
   - Transparent accounting practices
   - Potential revenue sources if needed (donations, premium features, sponsorships)

4. **Intellectual Property**
   - Content licensing (e.g., Creative Commons)
   - Rights and obligations of contributors
   - Ownership of documentation platform and brand

### Maintenance Role Framework

1. **Rotating Responsibilities**

   - Define critical maintenance tasks (content reviews, technical upkeep, community engagement)
   - Create rotation schedule to prevent burnout among core team

2. **Succession Planning**

   - Document processes thoroughly for knowledge transfer
   - Cross-train core team members on all critical functions
   - Identify potential future leaders early

3. **Review Cadence**
   - Schedule regular content freshness reviews (monthly/quarterly)
   - Define triggers for urgent updates (major ecosystem changes)

### Contribution Process

1. **Tiered Contribution Model**

   - First-time contributors (supervised contributions)
   - Regular contributors (less oversight required)
   - Trusted contributors (can approve others' work)

2. **Clear Acceptance Criteria**

   - Evidence-based recommendations (with required citation standards)
   - Style guide compliance
   - Community benefit threshold

3. **Contribution Workflow**
   - Proposal stage (pitch before substantial work)
   - Draft stage (collaborative development)
   - Review stage (peer and oversight review)
   - Publication stage (final approval and deployment)

### Scaling Framework

1. **Team Structure**

   - Subject/domain-based teams vs. function-based teams
   - Defining team lead responsibilities vs. oversight responsibilities

2. **Documentation Modularity**

   - Segmenting content domains to allow focused team expertise
   - Maintaining cross-references and consistency across domains

3. **Knowledge Transfer Systems**
   - Mentorship programs for new team leads
   - Documentation of meta-processes (how we document)
   - Regular cross-team information sharing
