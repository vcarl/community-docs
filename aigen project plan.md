# Community Documentation Project Plan

## 1. Project Overview

**Mission Statement**: Create and maintain a community-driven "source of truth" documentation that reflects the ecosystem's practical recommendations, even when they diverge from official guidelines.

**Scope**: A documentation resource serving a global community of millions, with direct input from an established Discord community of tens of thousands.

**Core Team**: 3-5 project overseers managing contributions from potentially hundreds of community members.

## 2. Project Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation and Planning (1-2 months)

- Define project governance structure and decision-making processes
- Establish contribution guidelines and content standards
- Create infrastructure for documentation (tech stack, hosting, etc.)
- Develop communication strategy for community engagement
- Outline initial documentation structure and priority topics

### Phase 2: Initial Content Development (2-3 months)

- Recruit initial content contributors from the community
- Develop first wave of core documentation topics
- Implement review processes with community feedback loops
- Create templates and style guides for consistent documentation
- Build initial version control and revision history systems

### Phase 3: Launch and Community Adoption (1 month)

- Soft launch to select community members for feedback
- Refine content and processes based on initial feedback
- Full public launch with community announcement
- Implement feedback gathering mechanisms
- Monitor initial community response and usage patterns

### Phase 4: Sustainable Maintenance (Ongoing)

- Establish regular review and update cadence
- Develop contributor recognition program
- Implement metrics to track documentation usage and value
- Create workflows for handling conflicting viewpoints
- Regular community engagement to expand contributor base

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### Core Oversight Team (3-5 people)

- Final approval authority for content
- Strategic direction and quality standards
- Conflict resolution and community management
- Liaison with platform owners when necessary
- Infrastructure and technical decisions

### Content Contributors

- Research and draft documentation on specific topics
- Participate in peer review processes
- Propose new content areas based on community needs
- Update existing documentation as ecosystem evolves

### Community Liaisons

- Gather feedback from broader community
- Identify trending topics or pain points needing documentation
- Advocate for documentation project within various community spaces
- Help recruit new contributors

## 4. Infrastructure and Tools

### Documentation Platform

- Collaborative editing environment (e.g., GitHub/GitLab with static site generator)
- Version control system
- Commenting and feedback mechanisms
- Search functionality
- Mobile-friendly design

### Communication Channels

- Dedicated Discord channels for the documentation project
- Regular community calls for synchronous discussion
- Asynchronous feedback mechanisms
- Transparent progress tracking visible to community

## 5. Content Strategy

### Priority Topic Identification

- Community surveys to identify highest-value documentation needs
- Analysis of frequently asked questions in community spaces
- Identification of areas with greatest divergence from official recommendations

### Content Structure

- Hierarchical organization with clear navigation
- Practical, solution-oriented approach
- Clear distinction between community consensus vs. varied approaches
- Explicit versioning to track changes over time

### Content Standards

- Evidence-based recommendations with citations
- Clear labeling of consensus levels (established practice vs. emerging approach)
- Inclusive language and accessibility considerations
- Transparent about methodology and decision processes

## 6. Managing Relationship with Product Owners

### Proactive Approach

- Clearly communicate project goals and non-adversarial stance
- Invite constructive feedback from product owners
- Establish clear differentiation between official and community documentation
- Document areas of alignment alongside divergence

### Conflict Mitigation

- Focus on use cases and practical outcomes rather than criticism
- Maintain professional tone in all documentation
- Establish process for handling potential cease and desist or copyright concerns
- Develop contingency plans for potential platform changes or restrictions

## 7. Sustainability Plan

### Community Engagement

- Regular recognition of contributors
- Documentation-focused events or sprints
- Integration with existing community rituals and gatherings
- Clear pathways for new contributors to get involved

### Knowledge Transfer

- Cross-training among core team members
- Documentation of meta-processes (how to maintain the documentation)
- Succession planning for core roles

### Measuring Success

- Usage metrics (visits, search terms, time on page)
- Community sentiment analysis
- Contribution velocity and contributor retention
- Impact on frequently asked questions in community spaces

## 8. Risk Management

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product owner pushback | High | Medium | Maintain professional tone, focus on value-add, establish clear disclaimers |
| Contributor burnout | Medium | High | Implement rotation systems, recognize contributions, manage scope |
| Content becoming outdated | High | High | Regular review cycles, automated flagging of aging content |
| Community disagreement on recommendations | Medium | High | Clear consensus labeling, transparent decision processes |
| Technical infrastructure issues | Medium | Low | Backup systems, distributed repositories, regular exports |

## 9. Timeline and Milestones

### Month 1-2: Foundation

- Complete project governance documentation
- Infrastructure setup complete
- Initial contributor team assembled
- Topic priority list established

### Month 3-4: Content Development

- 25% of priority topics with complete first drafts
- Review processes tested and refined
- 75% of priority topics with complete first drafts

### Month 5: Launch Preparation

- All priority topics reviewed and approved
- Navigation and search functionality tested
- Community feedback mechanisms in place

### Month 6: Launch and Initial Operations

- Public launch
- Establish regular update cadence
- First major community feedback collection

### Quarterly thereafter

- Content review cycle
- Contributor recognition
- Metrics analysis and strategic adjustments
